## What this says about the year

Grade 12 is **two halves**. Terms 1 and 2 are a run of short, sharp, varied
briefs — a laptop, a desk lamp, a Rube Goldberg machine, an ornament, a Mars
colony, a bunker house. Terms 3 and 4 are one thing: the Senior Capstone,
week-numbered rather than term-numbered, with its own journal, research log and
order form.

That is a different shape from grade 11, where projects run all four terms and
the capstone is only the last few weeks. The site should show that difference
rather than forcing both years into the same template.

Three named tracks recur that grade 11 does not have: **Festo MecLabs**,
**VEX AIM Bot**, and **Universal Robots e-Learning** — all three are the
external platforms already on the training page.
