Three font options

Same page, same colours, three typefaces. The palette is now taken straight out of the shop logo — purple #8d63ab, navy #262b39, silver #c4c4c4, and the bulb cream kept in reserve. Tell me A, B or C and I will stamp it across the whole site; it is one line to change, so switching later costs nothing. Try it in both light and dark mode if you can.

checking…Option ASpace Grotesk + Literata

Geometric and slightly technical. Space Grotesk has odd, memorable letterforms — the kind of face used on engineering and science publications. Literata is Google’s reading typeface, built specifically for long text on screens.

Designing for print

Parts that have to move need clearance built in — around 0.3 mm between surfaces that touch. Get it wrong in CAD and the print fuses solid, which is a lesson everyone learns exactly once. The 0.3 mm figure is a starting point, not a law.
