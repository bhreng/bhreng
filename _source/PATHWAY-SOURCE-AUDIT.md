## What this means

The deep-research feature answered "what is this discipline" rather than
"what should a high school student explore". It worked well where the field
has strong public teaching material and local regulation (Architecture,
Robotics) and poorly where the field's public documents are standards
(Software, Project Management).

The fix is not to discard breadth — the hubs are for self-discovery and depth
is fine. It is:

1. **Drop what does not open.** Paywalled or dead links fail a student
   immediately. *Modern Robotics*, the ResearchGate PDFs, the ERROR entry.
2. **Drop what is off-topic.** Summer bridge retention studies are not
   project management.
3. **Drop the SOLIDWORKS video.** Autodesk only.
4. **Tag by level** — start here / going deeper / college level — so breadth
   stays but a student knows what they are opening.
5. **Top up Electrical, Software and Project Management** with material aimed
   at the age group. Software is the urgent one: three usable sources.
