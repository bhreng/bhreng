## What this tells us about the course shape

**The weekly rhythm is fixed.** Every single week carries an "End of the Week
Tasks" assignment and a daily logbook entry per day. That is the scaffolding;
everything else is content dropped into it.

**Two naming conventions collide.** "End of the Week Tasks" and "End of Week
Tasks" both exist, sometimes in the same topic (Term 2 Week 1, Term 1 Week 2).
That is copy-forward drift, and it is the same disease as the duplicate logbook
template.

**The projects, in teaching order:**

1. Speaker Design (Term 1 Wk 1) — with safety test and acknowledgement first
2. Roles of an Engineer (Term 1 Wk 2)
3. Full Scope Project — Vision Statement, then Weeks 1–3 (Term 1 Wk 2–4)
4. Tiny House in Fusion 360 (Term 1 Wk 3)
5. ADU Design Project (Term 2 Wk 1)
6. Intro to ESEC — Arduino (Term 2 Wk 2)
7. Intro to Coreldraw (Term 2 Wk 3)
8. VEX V5 Clawbot (Term 2 Wk 3)
9. Simple Machines to Functional Mechanisms (Term 2 Wk 4)
10. Robotic Arm Build + Elegoo Uno Project Kit (Term 2 Wk 5)
11. Fusion 360 Animation (Term 3 Wk 1)
12. Client Desk Organizer (Term 3 Wk 1)
13. Creative Concept Design (Term 3 Wk 2)
14. City Design (Term 3 Wk 3–4)
15. Learning Revit! (Term 3 Wk 4)
16. Famous Architect Presentation (Term 4 Wk 1)
17. Bridge Conceptual Design Model (Term 4 Wk 1)
18. Grade 11 Capstone, four parts (EOY)
19. Vibecoding Team Challenge (Term 4 Wk 5)
20. Gmetrix + Reflection Portfolio Presentation (Final)

That is a genuine arc: safety → an approachable build → a full design process →
CAD depth → electronics and robotics → architecture → capstone → certification.
It maps onto the site's pathway structure far better than I expected.

**The Mid-Project Design Review recurs** on ADU, Client Desk Organizer, City
Design and the EOY project — confirming the template harvested from Drive is
standard practice, not occasional.

**Do Nows number roughly 20 in this class alone**, and several are titled by
tool rather than date: Forma #1, Forma #2, Adobe Neo, AI Help for Capstone,
Recreate House plans, New Forma Design tools, Brilliant. Those titles are
already better skill-library entries than the date-only ones.
