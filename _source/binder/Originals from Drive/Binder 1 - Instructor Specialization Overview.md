Our Engineering Technology program benefits from two distinct yet complementary instructional specializations, led by Mr. Frank (EDF) and Mr. Dryer (ESEC). This dual expertise ensures that students receive in-depth guidance across the full spectrum of the engineering lifecycle, from conceptual design and physical fabrication to digital systems integration and cutting-edge theoretical analysis.

While both instructors collaborate to support all student projects, our specialized roles allow students to seek targeted knowledge and mentorship based on their project needs and individual career interests.

## 1. Mr. Frank: Engineering Design & Fabrication (EDF)

- **Core Focus:** Mastering the art and science of **transforming ideas into tangible forms.** This specialization emphasizes the visual, structural, and manufacturable aspects of engineering, preparing students to create and build physical products and structures.
- **Key Expertise Areas (KC Focus):**
    - **Industrial & Aesthetic Design:** Principles of user-centered design, form, ergonomics, and visual impact.
    - **Technical Communication & Documentation:** Advanced CAD modeling, drafting standards (GD&T), architectural drawing, structural representations, and creating detailed "as-built" documentation.
    - **Physical Prototyping & Manufacturing:** Theory and practice of **Design for Manufacturing (DFM)**, specifically for additive (3D printing) and subtractive (laser engraving, CNC machining) fabrication processes.
    - **Structural & Construction Principles:** Fundamentals of structural integrity, material properties for construction, and civil engineering concepts.
- **Students will seek Mr. Frank for guidance on:**
    - Developing aesthetic and functional designs in CAD.
    - Optimizing parts for 3D printing or laser engraving.
    - Understanding how to construct robust and compliant structures.
    - Creating professional engineering drawings and documentation.
    - The iterative process of refining physical prototypes.
- **EDP Stages Emphasized:** *Develop Solutions* (form-driven), *Plan a Solution* (physical build), *Create* (fabrication), *Improve & Redesign* (physical iterations), *Present Your Solution* (visual communication).
- **Relevant Career Paths:** Product Design, Industrial Design, Architectural Engineering, Mechanical Engineering (Design-focused), Manufacturing Engineering, Construction Management, Technical Drafting.

## 2. Mr. Dryer: Engineering Systems & Emerging Concepts (ESEC)

- **Core Focus:** Understanding and developing the **intelligence, functionality, and control** that drive modern engineered systems. This specialization delves into the digital, electrical, and analytical foundations, preparing students to create smart, responsive, and innovative technologies.
- **Key Expertise Areas (KC Focus):**
    - **Digital Systems & Controls:** Electrical engineering fundamentals, digital electronics, logic design, programming (Arduino, Python, PLC), control systems, and mechatronics.
    - **Algorithmic Analysis & Software Engineering:** Developing efficient code, understanding software architecture, and applying computational thinking to engineering problems.
    - **Applied Research & Emerging Technologies:** Exploration of quantum computing, Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning in control systems, Internet of Things (IoT) principles, and identifying future technological trends.
    - **System Analysis & Mechanical Functionality:** Advanced materials science for functional application, **Finite Element Analysis (FEA) theory** for predictive performance, and the principles of **mechanical systems** (kinematics, dynamics, actuation).
