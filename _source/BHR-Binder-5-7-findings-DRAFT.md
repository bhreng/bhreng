# BHR Engineering Technology Binder — Sections 5, 6, 7

**Findings and rewrite plan · September 2, 2026**

I read all three in full before writing. The conclusion changed what I planned to do, so this document leads with why rather than with rewritten prose.

---

## Why these three aren't a straight rewrite

**Section 7 shouldn't be reworded at all.** It is a skill-by-skill map of DESE Standards 3–10 onto specific units — roughly 130 rows carrying real skill IDs (S3-a, S5-n, S9-ak, S10-cc) with the state's own wording. That wording is DESE's, not yours, and rewriting it would break the alignment it exists to prove. It's the single most valuable document in the binder and the only one that ties the program to the framework at skill level. It needs corrections and additions, not prose work.

**Section 5 is largely a copy of Sections 1–4.** Its §2 restates Section 1 (instructor specializations), §3 restates Section 2 (pillars), §4 restates Section 3 (PCCs), and §6 restates Section 4 (task classification) — in several places verbatim. Roughly a third of Section 5 is content the reader has already passed through, since the numbers are reading order.

That leaves Section 6 and the genuinely new parts of Section 5 — grading policy, documentation templates, resources, safety rules, classroom expectations — as the real rewrite targets.

---

## Corrections — Section 5

**1. "PCC 12" does not exist.** Grade 12 Unit 3 cites *"PCC 12 (Advanced Elective Engineering Area / Shared)"*. There are ten PCCs. This is **Standard 12** (Advanced Elective Area) mislabelled as a PCC. A reader checking against the PCC list finds nothing there.

**2. The senior capstone has three different durations across the binder.**

| Section | Says |
|---|---|
| Section 5, Grade 12 Unit 5 | "A year-long project" |
| Section 6, §6.2 | "A 35-day, half-year project" |
| Section 7, §3.2 | "a 35-day, self-directed project" |

Two say 35 days; Section 5 says year-long. Section 5 is the outlier and presumably the error.

**3. Four names for one document.** Within Section 5 alone the daily documentation instrument is called:

- "Engineering Notebooks" (§3.5, A&R activities)
- "Engineering Journal" (Grade 9 Unit 6)
- "Engineering Daily Journal" (§7.3 and the §9.3 template)

The actual instrument is the **Engineering Logbook**. Section 7 adds a fourth usage, "Engineering Daily Journal," in §4.0.

⚠ **One wrinkle worth your judgment.** Standard S3-e, in DESE's own words, reads: *"Maintain an **engineering journal** for projects to document design solutions."* So "journal" is the state's term. Your rename to "logbook" is a deliberate local choice that now diverges from DESE language — which cuts against the rule we set. My recommendation: keep "Engineering Logbook" as the instrument's name since that's what's on the template students use, but state explicitly, once, that it satisfies S3-e's engineering journal requirement. That way the divergence is documented rather than accidental.

**4. The §9.3 documentation template is stale.** It describes the old structure — Project Title, Tools Used, Daily Bullet Points, Notes (~50+ words), Next Class's Agenda, Images. The current logbook is built around **three intervals** (prior to break, break to lunch, lunch to end of day), each with an EDP status code, work highlights, roadblocks, and visual evidence, closing with challenges, a daily synthesis, and next class's agenda. The appendix template needs replacing with the real one.

**5. Section 5 and Section 4 disagree about the activity list — and Section 5 is the newer thinking.** Section 4 lists **Independent Study**. Section 5 lists **Independent Focus** in the same slot, with the same definition ("a self-directed, long-term project where students explore a chosen area in-depth").

This is significant beyond terminology: **it tells us what the Independent Focus actually is inside the pillar framework.** The Independent Focus is the renamed Independent Study — Applied Learning, self-directed, term-length. That single fact does more to explain the hub project than any of the May 2026 program reports. Section 7 §4.0 still says "Independent Study Reflections"; Section 5 §7.3 says "Independent Focus Reflections."

**6. Heading numbering skips.** §4.0 → 4.1 → 4.2 → 4.3 → **4.5**. No §4.4.

**7. Markdown escape artifacts throughout the tables.** Literal `\*\*Mr. Frank:\*\*`, `S\&O`, `Do Now\!`, `<br>` appearing as text. These are paste artifacts from the generating tool and are visible to readers in the Docs tables. Present in Sections 5, 6 and 7.

## Corrections — Section 6

**1. "Engineering Journal/Notebook"** appears in Grade 9 Unit 6. Same terminology fix.

**2. "Dual-axis framework"** returns in §2.0. Invented vocabulary; the sentence works better as what it actually means: the five pillars describe *how* students learn, the ten PCCs describe *what* they learn.

**3. Grade 12 has five units where Grades 9–11 have eight.** Defensible, since the 35-day capstone consumes half the year — but the asymmetry should be stated rather than left for the reader to notice and wonder about.

**4. Otherwise Section 6 is in good shape.** The grade-by-grade progression is clear, the unit tables are concrete, and the capstone descriptions are specific. This one needs a light pass, not surgery.

---

## Corrections — Section 7

**1. Only Standards 3–10 are mapped. Standards 1, 2, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 15 are absent entirely.**

The crosswalk names 1, 2, 3, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 as the "Universal Program Standards" — the non-negotiable core every student gets. Seven of those eight have no unit mapping anywhere.

**Standard 1 (Safety and Health) is the one I would fix first.** Grade 10 has an entire OSHA certification unit, the safety rules run three pages in Section 5's appendix, and S&O is a whole pillar — yet the standard covering all of it appears nowhere in the map. For a vocational program in an inspection context, safety is the standard you least want undocumented.

Standards 13 (Employability) and 12 (Advanced Elective Area) are similarly load-bearing: the Weekly Grade and Employability categories are 50% of the course grade between them, and the Independent Focus is built on Standard 12.

**2. Several units have no standards mapped.** Grade 9 Units 1 and 8; Grade 10 Units 1, 4, 8; Grade 11 Units 1, 3, 8; Grade 12 Units 1 and 3.

Review and skill-reinforcement units being unmapped is reasonable. But **Grade 9's EOY Project, Grade 10's EOY Capstone, and Grade 10's OSHA unit** are substantial graded work with no standards attached. The OSHA unit in particular maps directly to Standard 1 — it is unmapped only because Standard 1 isn't in the report's scope.

**3. Title mismatch.** The document's internal title is "BHR Engineering Technology: Curriculum Report"; the file is named "Binder 7: Curriculum Standards Analysis Report."

**4. §5.0 asserts credentials as established.** OSHA 10, ACU, and CSWA are named as credentials the curriculum "directly prepares students to achieve." Consistent with the rest of the program documents, this needs the planned-versus-running distinction applied.

## What I propose to build

1. **Section 7 gets extended, not rewritten.** Add mappings for Standards 1, 2, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 against the units that already teach them, and attach standards to the unmapped project units. This turns a Standards 3–10 report into a complete framework map — and it's the piece most likely to matter if anyone from the district asks how the program meets the frameworks. I'd want the 2025 DESE PDF read first so the added skill IDs are real rather than inferred.

2. **Section 5 gets restructured, not just reworded.** Sections 2, 3, 4 and 6 of it become short cross-references to Binder Sections 1–4 rather than full restatements. The genuinely unique material — grading policy, weekly rubric, documentation templates, resources, safety rules, classroom expectations — stays and gets the prose pass. This is the one place I'd deviate from "same structure," because the duplication is the structure problem. **Say the word and I'll leave it as a straight restatement instead.**

3. **Section 6 gets a light prose pass** and the terminology fixes.

4. **A terminology sweep across all eight sections** — one table listing every retired term, its replacement, and every location it still appears. Notebook/journal/logbook is the big one, Independent Study / Independent Focus is the second.

## Decisions needed

1. **Section 5 restructure — yes or no?** Cross-reference Sections 1–4 instead of restating them, or keep the duplication.
2. **Extend Section 7 to all fifteen standards?** This is the highest-value work available, and it needs the 2025 DESE PDF read to do properly.
3. **"Engineering Logbook" vs DESE's "engineering journal"** — confirm the local name stays and gets an explicit note tying it to S3-e.
4. **Independent Study or Independent Focus?** Section 5 says Independent Focus, Sections 4 and 7 say Independent Study. Which is current?
5. **Senior capstone: 35 days.** Confirm, and I'll correct Section 5.
