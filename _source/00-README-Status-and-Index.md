## Vocabulary rule in force

**DESE language wins over local coinage.** Invented terms with no state equivalent are replaced with plain description. Two exceptions, both deliberate:

- **"Engineering Accounting"** stays — it is a real term from *Foundations of Engineering* (Holtzapple & Reece), a core textbook, not invented jargon. It now carries a citation on first use.
- **"Engineering Logbook"** stays, despite DESE saying "engineering journal," because it is the name on the template students actually use. The mapping to S3-e is stated explicitly.
