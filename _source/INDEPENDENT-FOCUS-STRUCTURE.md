# Independent Focus — how it runs

**Current as of 05 Sep 2026.** This supersedes `EEP-TERM-STRUCTURE-PROPOSAL.md`
(v1–v4), which is kept in `_to_delete/` for the history. Everything here is
either Dan's stated position or is built and on the site. The two things still
open are marked.

---

## The words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Independent Focus** | The whole thing, across a student's years in the shop. You pick one each term. Out loud: **your focus**. |
| **Engineering Pathway** | The field picked for the term. Seven supported; others by approval for upperclassmen, who then do their own research. |
| **Pathway Hub** | Where that pathway's resources live on the site. |
| **Independent Project** | What is made or done this term. **Not graded.** |
| **Independent Research** | The finding-out end of the same work. Some terms this *is* the work. |

"E.E.P." / "Elective Engineering Pathway" and "Independent Study" are retired.
"Independent Engineering Pathway" was rejected because IEP means something
else in a school.

## The rules, as stated by Dan

- Every student in the shop has a focus. Mr. Dryer runs it for Grades 9 and 10 and may frame it his own way.
- A home pathway is picked **each term**. Same one every term to go deep; a different one each term to come out broad; anything between.
- A term can start something new or **carry the last one forward**. Set a goal for the term, hit it, pick it up next term.
- The hub follows the part of the problem you are on — the radio example: Term 1 Electrical for the circuit, Term 2 Industrial Design for the enclosure.
- **The Independent Project is not graded.** Documentation and the term requirements are, and they sit beside the project. Regular projects and assignments are where work itself gets marked.
- The focus **ends when the Senior Capstone starts** (Grade 12 Term 3). It may become the capstone; it need not be connected to it.
- The seven pathways are the supported list. An upperclassman may pick a field outside them with approval, and is then responsible for their own research.

## The calendar it runs in

180 school days. Shop week is all five days, full day; the following week is
academics with no shop. A term is about ten calendar weeks, about five shop
weeks, about **twenty-five full shop days**. What is short is not the term but
continuity — every project goes cold for a week, every week — which is why
the logbook's "next class" box matters more than it looks.

## What exists now

| Artifact | Where |
|---|---|
| **Independent Focus Term Sheet** (.docx) — Declare on day one, Reflect on the last day | `student-docs/`, site Documents page, both grade-page focus entries |
| **Independent Focus Record** (.pdf) — one row per term, six rows plus a capstone row, kept in the binder | same |
| The Daily Logbook — the running record underneath everything | same |
| The seven pathway hubs, each with linked downloads | site |
| The words, the rules and the radio example, in student language | site, pathways page |

The Term Sheet's Reflect half restores the term-end reflection the binder
specifies (Section 5: project title, equipment, tasks completed, skills learned,
status, challenges and adaptations, project management, goals for next term).
The Declare half is new, and small — pathway, why, one line of intent — so
that the reflection has something to compare against.

There is **no weekly journal** for the focus. The logbook covers it; a
separate journal was built and retired.
