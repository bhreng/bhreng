Every shop day

The two you never skip

Daily LogbookDOCXThe running record of every shop day. Print it or keep it in Drive.

Monday and Friday of a shop week

Plan it, then say what happened

Weekly PlannerDOCXWhat you intend to get done this shop week, before the week starts. Weekly ReflectionDOCXThe Friday counterpart to the planner: what actually happened.

When a project starts

Fix what “finished” means before you touch anything

Design Brief and Initial PlannerDOCXThe document that fixes what “finished” means, before you start. Decision MatrixXLSXA spreadsheet. Weighted criteria against your concepts; the totals decide. Project Gantt ChartXLSXA spreadsheet. Tasks on a calendar, to find where two of them collide. Part ListXLSXA spreadsheet. Every part including the fasteners, made or bought. Order Request FormXLSXA spreadsheet. The “why you need it” column is the one that gets it approved.

While you build and test

The evidence

Test and Measurement LogXLSXA spreadsheet. Predicted, measured, and the difference — one row per reading. I/O Map and Commissioning ChecklistXLSXA spreadsheet. Every input and output on the system, and the checklist you commission it against. Research LogXLSXA spreadsheet. One new source a day is the target. Mid-Project Design ReviewDOCXPartway through: where you are, and the one thing most likely to fail. Instructor Meeting NotesDOCXAfter a one-on-one. Action items with names and dates on them.

When something ends

Short, and where the grade comes from

Project ReflectionDOCXAt the end of a project. What worked, what you would change. Do Now ReflectionDOCXThe short write-up after a Do Now session.

Your Independent Focus

A proposal, a reflection, and one page for two years

Independent Focus ProposalDOCXFirst day of the term. The idea, the work, the five weeks, and a review box for your instructor. Independent Focus ReflectionDOCXLast day of the term. What got done, what changed, and where next term starts. Independent Focus RecordPDFOne page, one row per term, kept in your binder. The shape of what you chose across two years.
