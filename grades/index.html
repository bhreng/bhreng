How the four years fit together

Grade 9 is one exploratory week. Grade 10 is the year the shop becomes yours — certifications, OSHA, and a real documentation standard. Grade 11 is the widest year, with projects across every pathway. Grade 12 narrows again: two terms of sharp briefs, then one capstone you choose and run yourself.

Who teaches what. Mr. Dryer delivers Grades 9 and 10; Mr. Frank delivers Grades 11 and 12. That is about whose Classroom the work comes from, not about who you can ask — both are in the shop with all four years, and the pathways page says which of them to go to for which kind of problem.
